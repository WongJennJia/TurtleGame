Bean Values

Black Bean = RM5

Peanut = RM2

Soya Bean = RM1

Red Bean = 50 cents

Green Bean = 10 cents

📌 Pin = -RM5 (Medical Expense)

Game Rules

  • Collect as many beans as possible in 1 minute
  • Each bean has a different value (see left)
  • Avoid pins - they deduct RM5 from your total
  • Use arrow keys to move your turtle
  • Press SPACE to create a gust of wind
  • Wind will slowly move items on the board
  • Player with the highest savings after deductions wins
  • Tiebreaker: Player with fewer pins wins

How to Play

  • Player 1: Arrow Keys + Spacebar
  • Player 2: W,A,S,D + Q Key
  • Player 3: I,J,K,L + U Key
  • Player 4: Numpad 8,4,6,2 + Numpad 0

Note: Food items and pins will move slowly on their own due to wind currents.
